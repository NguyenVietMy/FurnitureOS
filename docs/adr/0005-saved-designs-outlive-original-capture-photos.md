# Explicit saved copies outlive original Capture photos

Save consent retains an immutable Design and minimal confirmed Capture metadata until that save is deleted. Original Capture drafts and canonical photos keep their existing 24-hour expiry; original-Capture deletion removes those originals while explicitly saved copies remain independently manageable. This permits later Design recovery without silently extending private-photo retention or turning archived metadata into upload, analysis or live-Capture authority.

Deleting a save immediately erases its content and revokes its restored sessions. A minimal consumed-key marker protects the retry horizon for 25 hours to prevent a lost-response retry from recreating deleted content; pending retries last at most 24 hours and source sessions at most 30 minutes. After 25 hours the marker becomes eligible for lazy removal during that owner's next new save, so physical marker retention can be longer when the owner does not return. No scheduled marker-removal deadline is promised. Linking a verified email retains the same owner UUID; returning to another account is a deliberate switch, with no automatic merge or ownership transfer.
